  story.mp3     "Dragon's Lullaby"     — Scott Buckley
  victory.mp3   "Simulacra"            — Scott Buckley

Original filenames as downloaded: descent.mp3, SongOfTheForge.mp3,
DragonsLullaby2023.mp3, Simulacra.mp3.

To swap which track plays where, just rename the files. The game looks for
exactly these four names in this folder:

  explore.mp3   plays on the map, the storyline pages and during exercises
  battle.mp3    plays during boss fights
  story.mp3     plays on the story chapter screens
  victory.mp3   plays on the chapter you get after beating a titan

If a file is missing or cannot be decoded, the game falls back to its own
generated score automatically. Nothing breaks.

NOTE: these MP3s only play when game.html is opened from this computer.
The published web version blocks audio loaded from outside the page, so it
always uses the generated score.
